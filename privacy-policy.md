# SawPilot Fusion – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

SawPilot Fusion is an add-in for Autodesk Fusion developed by Mahmoud Sayed.

## Information We Collect

SawPilot Fusion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, Autodesk Fusion model geometry, design files, or usage analytics.

SawPilot Fusion does not use advertising trackers, analytics services, or telemetry services.

## Local Processing

SawPilot Fusion processes model information locally within Autodesk Fusion in order to provide its woodworking analysis and workshop preparation features.

Workshop and session information required for features such as workflow resume is stored locally within the Autodesk Fusion environment.

## Generated Files

Workshop print and output files generated by SawPilot Fusion are created locally on the user's computer. SawPilot Fusion does not upload these files to an external server.

## Autodesk Fusion

SawPilot Fusion operates within Autodesk Fusion. Autodesk products and services may process information independently according to Autodesk's own privacy terms.

This Privacy Policy applies only to data processing performed by SawPilot Fusion itself.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the data practices of SawPilot Fusion change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
