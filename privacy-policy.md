# SawPilot Fusion – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes data processing performed by SawPilot Fusion, an add-in for Autodesk Fusion. Autodesk products and the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace operate under Autodesk's own terms and privacy practices.

## 2. Data collected by SawPilot Fusion

SawPilot Fusion does not collect or transmit personal information, Autodesk Fusion model geometry, design files, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or telemetry to the publisher or to an external SawPilot server.

SawPilot Fusion does not use advertising trackers or analytics services.

## 3. Local processing and purpose

SawPilot Fusion reads geometry from the active Autodesk Fusion design locally to provide woodworking cut-angle analysis, edge dimensions, face analysis, workshop navigation, print preparation, and related workshop features.

Model geometry used for these functions is processed locally within Autodesk Fusion and is not uploaded by SawPilot Fusion.

## 4. Local workshop recovery data

To support Workshop Resume, SawPilot Fusion stores a small recovery snapshot as an Autodesk Fusion attribute in the active design.

The snapshot contains SawPilot piece identifiers and workshop progress such as the current piece and completed-piece history. It does not contain a copy of model geometry or personal profile information.

The recovery snapshot is retained with the design for the purpose of resuming the workshop.

Choosing **Start New Workshop** clears the previous SawPilot recovery snapshot before starting a new workshop state.

Users who no longer want SawPilot data associated with a design can start a new workshop to clear the prior recovery state and then remove or disable SawPilot. For complete removal of any newly-created workshop state, do not save subsequent SawPilot workshop progress into that design.

## 5. Generated files

Workshop print and output files requested by the user are generated locally on the user's computer.

SawPilot Fusion does not upload those files to an external SawPilot service.

The user controls retention and deletion of generated files through the computer's normal file-management tools.

## 6. Third parties

SawPilot Fusion does not send data collected by the add-in to third-party analytics, advertising, telemetry, or SawPilot cloud services.

Therefore, SawPilot Fusion does not appoint third parties to process such data on its behalf.

Autodesk Fusion and Autodesk Marketplace may process information independently under Autodesk's own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Consent, revocation, access, and deletion

SawPilot Fusion does not request consent for publisher-side collection because it does not collect or transmit personal data to the publisher.

Users may stop SawPilot Fusion processing at any time by closing or disabling the add-in.

Local generated files can be deleted by the user.

Workshop recovery data is local to the Fusion design as described above.

Privacy questions or deletion requests concerning data held by the publisher may be sent to the support address below. Because SawPilot Fusion does not maintain a publisher-side personal-data database, there is ordinarily no SawPilot server record to delete.

## 8. Changes

If SawPilot Fusion's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.
